# National Immunization Schedule (NIS) — India & Bihar Health Guidelines

## Overview
The Universal Immunization Programme (UIP) in India protects infants, children, and pregnant women against 12 vaccine-preventable diseases. Under the National Health Mission (NHM) and Bihar State Health Society, ASHA and ANM workers are responsible for maintaining the village Due List and organizing regular Village Health, Sanitation and Nutrition Days (VHSND).

## Immunization for Pregnant Women
- **Td 1 (Tetanus and adult Diphtheria)**: Early in pregnancy as soon as pregnancy is detected.
- **Td 2**: 4 weeks after Td 1.
- **Td Booster**: If a woman received 2 Td doses in a pregnancy within the last 3 years, only 1 booster dose is required.
- **Dose & Route**: 0.5 ml, Intramuscular (IM), upper arm.

## Immunization for Infants
### At Birth (Within 24 hours of birth)
1. **BCG (Bacillus Calmette-Guérin)**:
   - Target: Protection against tuberculosis (especially tuberculous meningitis and miliary TB).
   - Dose & Route: 0.05 ml (until 1 month of age) or 0.1 ml (after 1 month up to 1 year), Intradermal (ID), left upper arm.
2. **Hepatitis B (Birth Dose)**:
   - Must be given within 24 hours of birth to prevent vertical transmission.
   - Dose & Route: 0.5 ml, Intramuscular (IM), anterolateral aspect of mid-thigh.
3. **OPV 0 (Oral Polio Vaccine)**:
   - Dose & Route: 2 drops, Oral.

### At 6 Weeks
1. **OPV 1**: 2 drops, Oral.
2. **Pentavalent 1 (DTP + Hepatitis B + Hib)**:
   - Protects against Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus, Hepatitis B, and Haemophilus influenzae type b.
   - Dose & Route: 0.5 ml, IM, anterolateral mid-thigh (left side).
3. **Rotavirus Vaccine (RVV 1)**:
   - Protects against rotavirus diarrhea.
   - Dose & Route: 5 drops (or 2.5 ml depending on brand), Oral.
4. **Fractional IPV 1 (fIPV 1)**:
   - Fractional Inactivated Poliovirus Vaccine.
   - Dose & Route: 0.1 ml, Intradermal (ID), right upper arm.
5. **PCV 1 (Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine)**:
   - Protects against pneumococcal pneumonia and meningitis.
   - Dose & Route: 0.5 ml, IM, anterolateral mid-thigh (right side).

### At 10 Weeks
1. **OPV 2**: 2 drops, Oral.
2. **Pentavalent 2**: 0.5 ml, IM, anterolateral mid-thigh (left side).
3. **Rotavirus Vaccine (RVV 2)**: Oral drops.

### At 14 Weeks
1. **OPV 3**: 2 drops, Oral.
2. **Pentavalent 3**: 0.5 ml, IM, anterolateral mid-thigh (left side).
3. **Rotavirus Vaccine (RVV 3)**: Oral drops.
4. **Fractional IPV 2 (fIPV 2)**: 0.1 ml, ID, right upper arm.
5. **PCV 2**: 0.5 ml, IM, anterolateral mid-thigh (right side).

### At 9 Completed Months (Up to 12 Months)
1. **MR 1 (Measles & Rubella)**:
   - Protects against measles and congenital rubella syndrome.
   - Dose & Route: 0.5 ml, Subcutaneous (SC), right upper arm.
2. **JE 1 (Japanese Encephalitis)**:
   - Administered in endemic districts in Bihar (e.g., Gaya, Patna, Muzaffarpur).
   - Dose & Route: 0.5 ml, SC, left upper arm.
3. **PCV Booster**: 0.5 ml, IM, anterolateral mid-thigh.
4. **Vitamin A (1st Dose)**:
   - Dose: 1 ml (1 lakh International Units), Oral.

### At 16 to 24 Months
1. **MR 2 (Measles & Rubella second dose)**: 0.5 ml, SC.
2. **JE 2 (Japanese Encephalitis second dose)**: In endemic districts, 0.5 ml, SC.
3. **DPT Booster 1**: 0.5 ml, IM, anterolateral mid-thigh.
4. **OPV Booster**: 2 drops, Oral.
5. **Vitamin A (Doses 2 to 9)**: 2 ml (2 lakh IU) administered every 6 months up to 5 years of age.

### At 5 to 6 Years
1. **DPT Booster 2**: 0.5 ml, IM, upper arm.

### At 10 Years and 16 Years
1. **Td (Tetanus & adult Diphtheria)**: 0.5 ml, IM, upper arm.

## Key Adverse Events Following Immunization (AEFI) Advice
- Mild fever and local pain after Pentavalent/DPT are common; paracetamol syrup drops may be provided as per ANM prescription.
- A small swelling/papule after BCG is normal and should not be squeezed or massaged; it leaves a permanent scar after 6-12 weeks.
- In case of persistent high fever, convulsions, or severe allergic reaction, immediately refer to the nearest PHC/CHC.
