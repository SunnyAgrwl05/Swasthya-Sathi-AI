# ASHA & ANM Operational Protocols — Bihar Primary Health Network

## Roles and Responsibilities of ASHA Workers
ASHA (Accredited Social Health Activist) acts as the primary health interface between the community and the public healthcare system:
1. **Household Survey & Registration**:
   - Maintain the village survey register, tracking every family, eligible couples, pregnant women, newborns, and children under 5 years.
   - Update the Pregnancy and Child Tracking Due List every month before the VHSND session.
2. **Community Mobilization**:
   - Escort pregnant women to PHC/CHC for institutional delivery.
   - Mobilize beneficiaries for Village Health, Sanitation and Nutrition Day (VHSND / Gram Swasthya Swachhta Evam Poshan Diwas).
   - Mobilize children for routine immunization sessions and Pulse Polio campaigns.
3. **Home-Based Newborn Care (HBNC)**:
   - Schedule home visits after birth:
     - Institutional delivery: 6 visits on Days 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, and 42.
     - Home delivery: 7 visits on Days 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, and 42.
   - Examine newborn for birth weight, temperature, umbilical cord sepsis, jaundice, and exclusive breastfeeding.
4. **First Aid & Drug Depot Holder**:
   - Authorized to stock and dispense basic OTC kit: ORS packets, Zinc tablets, Paracetamol syrup/tablets, Iron-Folic Acid tablets, Chloroquine (in malaria zones), Condoms, and Oral Contraceptive Pills (Mala-N, Chhaya).
   - **Crucial Rule**: ASHAs are NOT authorized to prescribe or administer injectables, IV fluids, or prescription antibiotics without direct medical officer supervision.

## Village Health, Sanitation and Nutrition Day (VHSND) Protocol
- **Frequency**: Organized at least once a month at every Anganwadi center or village sub-center.
- **Key Joint Team**: ANM (clinical lead), ASHA (community mobilization), and Anganwadi Worker (nutrition, weight, and supplementary feeding).
- **Core Activities**:
  1. Routine immunization of all infants, children, and pregnant women on the Due List.
  2. Antenatal checkups (weight, BP measurement by ANM, abdominal examination, Td vaccination, and IFA distribution).
  3. Growth monitoring and identification of Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) children.
  4. Counseling on family planning methods (Antara injectable, Chhaya pills, Copper-T/IUCD, Condoms, and terminal sterilization).
  5. Adolescent health counseling and sanitary napkin distribution.

## Emergency Referral Protocol for PHC/CHC
When encountering severe complications (antepartum hemorrhage, obstructed labor, neonatal asphyxia, severe dehydration, snake bite):
1. **Immediate Action**: Call 102 (National Ambulance Service) or organize local transport.
2. **Escort**: ASHA must accompany the patient to the PHC/FRU with all available records (MCP card, past ANC logs).
3. **Handover**: Provide a verbal and written handover to the Medical Officer on duty.
