# Maternal, Antenatal & Neonatal Health Guidelines — Bihar NHM

## Antenatal Care (ANC) Protocol
All pregnant women must receive comprehensive antenatal care to identify and treat potential complications early.

### Mandatory ANC Checkups Schedule
1. **1st ANC Visit**: Within the 1st trimester (before 12 weeks of pregnancy) for early pregnancy registration, issuing the Mother and Child Protection (MCP) card, baseline hemoglobin, blood pressure, urine testing, and Td 1 vaccination.
2. **2nd ANC Visit**: Between 14 and 26 weeks (2nd trimester) for fetal growth monitoring, fundal height, maternal weight gain, and ultrasound if indicated.
3. **3rd ANC Visit**: At 28 to 34 weeks (3rd trimester) for identifying gestational diabetes, pregnancy-induced hypertension (preeclampsia), and malpresentation.
4. **4th ANC Visit**: Between 36 weeks and term to confirm fetal lie/presentation, birth preparedness, and institutional delivery planning.
- **Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA)**: Held on the 9th of every month at all PHCs/CHCs for dedicated check-ups by doctors and obstetricians.

### Nutrition and Micronutrient Supplementation
- **Folic Acid**: 400 mcg daily during the first trimester (first 12 weeks) to prevent neural tube defects.
- **Iron and Folic Acid (IFA) Tablets**:
  - Dose: 1 tablet daily (containing 60 mg elemental iron + 500 mcg folic acid) starting from the second trimester (after 14 weeks) for a minimum of 180 days during pregnancy, and continuing for 180 days postpartum during lactation.
  - Counseling: Take after food with water or lemon water; do not take with tea, coffee, or milk.
- **Calcium Supplementation**:
  - Dose: 500 mg elemental calcium + Vitamin D twice daily (total 1000 mg/day) starting from 14 weeks of pregnancy until 6 months postpartum.
  - Rule: Calcium and IFA must NEVER be swallowed together (maintain at least 2 hours gap).
- **Deworming**: 1 tablet of Albendazole (400 mg) single dose orally after the first trimester.

## High-Risk Pregnancy (HRP) Identification & Red Flags
Immediate referral to the PHC or First Referral Unit (FRU) is mandatory if any of the following danger signs are observed:
1. Severe anemia (Hemoglobin < 7 g/dL) or breathlessness at rest.
2. High blood pressure (Systolic BP >= 140 mmHg and/or Diastolic BP >= 90 mmHg).
3. Swelling of face, hands, or sudden weight gain (Preeclampsia warning).
4. Vaginal bleeding at any stage of pregnancy (threatened abortion or placenta praevia/abruptio).
5. Severe headache, blurred vision, or epigastric pain.
6. Convulsions or fits (Eclampsia).
7. Decreased or absent fetal movements after 24 weeks.
8. Premature leakage of amniotic fluid (PROM) or high fever with foul-smelling vaginal discharge.

## Government Welfare Schemes & Institutional Delivery
- **Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)**: Cash assistance for institutional delivery:
  - Rural areas in Bihar (Low Performing State): Rs. 1,400 for mother + Rs. 600 incentive for ASHA facilitator.
- **Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)**:
  - Entitles all pregnant women delivering in public health institutions to completely free delivery, free C-section, free medicines, free diagnostics, free diet during hospital stay, and free transport from home to hospital and back.
